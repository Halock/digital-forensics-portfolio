# Digital Forensics Case Studies

## Overview

The source portfolio documents five areas of digital forensic investigation. The descriptions below are intentionally generalized for public presentation and focus on the forensic and analytical capabilities demonstrated.

---

## Case Study 1 — Counter-Terrorism Device Exploitation

### Objective

Examine recovered mobile devices for digital artifacts relevant to an investigation.

### Forensic Focus

Analysis included:

* Contacts
* Messages
* Media files
* Communication records
* Communication patterns

### Analytical Outcome

Forensic analysis identified communication patterns across multiple regions and produced information that could support further investigative analysis.

### Skills Demonstrated

* Mobile device forensics
* Digital artifact recovery
* Communication analysis
* Intelligence reporting

---
