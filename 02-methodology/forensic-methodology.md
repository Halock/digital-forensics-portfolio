# Digital Forensic Methodology

## Overview

The digital forensic workflow documented in this portfolio focuses on the systematic examination of recovered mobile devices, SIM cards, and communication artifacts.

The process is designed to move from evidence acquisition through artifact analysis and communication analysis to structured intelligence reporting.

## 1. Evidence Identification

Potentially relevant digital evidence is identified from recovered devices and communication-related artifacts.

The examination may include:

* Mobile devices
* SIM cards
* Stored communications
* Contacts
* Media files
* Communication records

## 2. Forensic Acquisition

Digital evidence is acquired using specialized forensic acquisition technologies.

The documented toolkit includes:

* MSAB XRY
* SIM card forensic readers
* Mobile device forensic workstations

The objective is to obtain relevant digital evidence for subsequent examination while maintaining a structured forensic workflow.

## 3. Artifact Recovery

Following acquisition, relevant digital artifacts are recovered and organized for analysis.

Examples of artifacts documented in the source portfolio include:

* Contacts
* Messages
* Media files
* Communication records
* SIM card data

## 4. Artifact Analysis

Recovered artifacts are examined to identify information relevant to the investigation.

Analysis focuses on identifying:

* Communication relationships
* Relevant digital records
* Activity patterns
* Connections between individuals or entities
* Potential investigative leads

## 5. Communication Analysis

Communication artifacts and Call Data Records (CDRs) are analyzed to identify patterns in communication activity.

This analysis can help establish relationships and identify areas of activity relevant to an investigation.

The source portfolio documents the identification of **15 communication locations** through analysis.

## 6. Intelligence Analysis

Forensic findings are interpreted in an investigative and intelligence context.

The objective is not simply to recover data, but to convert relevant forensic findings into information that can assist investigators, analysts, and decision-makers.

## 7. Reporting

Findings are documented through structured intelligence reports.

Reports communicate relevant forensic observations and analytical results while maintaining appropriate handling of sensitive information.

## End-to-End Workflow

The documented methodology can be represented as:

**Evidence Identification → Forensic Acquisition → Artifact Recovery → Artifact Analysis → Communication Analysis → Intelligence Analysis → Reporting**
