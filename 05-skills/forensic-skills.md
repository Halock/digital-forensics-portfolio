# Digital Forensic Skills

## Overview

The documented investigations demonstrate practical capabilities across digital forensics, mobile device examination, communication analysis, intelligence development, and technical reporting.

---

## Mobile Device Forensics

Experience includes the forensic examination of recovered mobile devices and the identification of relevant digital artifacts.

**Capabilities include:**

* Mobile device examination
* Digital evidence acquisition
* Artifact recovery
* Artifact interpretation
* Evidence organization

---

## SIM Card Forensics

Experience includes the examination of SIM card data as part of broader mobile device investigations.

**Capabilities include:**

* SIM data extraction
* Contact examination
* Stored message examination
* SIM artifact analysis

---

## Digital Artifact Analysis

Recovered digital artifacts are examined systematically to identify information relevant to investigative objectives.

**Capabilities include:**

* Message analysis
* Contact analysis
* Media examination
* Communication record analysis
* Identification of relevant digital records

---

## Communication Analysis

Communication artifacts and related records can be analyzed to identify patterns, relationships, and areas of investigative interest.

**Capabilities include:**

* Communication pattern analysis
* Relationship identification
* Communication timeline analysis
* CDR analysis
* Identification of communication locations

---

## Intelligence Analysis

Forensic findings can be transformed into structured information that supports investigative and intelligence objectives.

**Capabilities include:**

* Analytical interpretation
* Pattern identification
* Relationship analysis
* Intelligence development
* Investigative support

---

## Technical Reporting

Forensic and analytical findings are documented through structured reports designed to communicate relevant observations and conclusions clearly.

**Capabilities include:**

## Investigative Support

Digital forensic findings can provide investigative teams with information relevant to ongoing examinations.

**Capabilities include:**
