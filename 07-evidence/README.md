# Forensic Evidence & Artifact Controls

## Public Portfolio Disclaimer
The case studies and associated files in this repository are presented at a generalized, sanitized level. 

To maintain operational security and preserve legal confidentiality:
* Specific target identities, suspect names, and cellular numbers are intentionally excluded.
* Exact operational coordinates and precise communication location logs are generalized.
* Raw device extractions, forensic image dumps, and protected physical evidence are strictly withheld from this public environment.
